Changes made for PinglyMotion: converted from Collada to PinglyMotion's own mesh
format (maxine.pmm) by tools/make_max_mesh.py. Only shape, normals and joint
weights are kept (no textures or UV maps). Weights on collision volumes were
moved to their bones, at most four joints per vertex are kept, and 393
vertices inside the mouth that had no weights got the weights of their
nearest weighted neighbour.
