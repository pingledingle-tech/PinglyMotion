A more approachable way to animate

Small movements.
Big possibilities.

Whether you're shaping a sitting pose or experimenting with a new motion, PinglyMotion is being built to make animation creation more accessible.

01 / POSE

Get hands-on.

Select and rotate Bento joints, including fingers, face, wings and tail. Work with keyframes, mirroring and joint limits.

02 / CREATE

Make it yours.

Open and edit animation files, explore different poses and save your work. Exported BVH is checked against Second Life upload rules.

03 / EXPLORE

Bring an assistant.

An optional local interface lets compatible AI tools pose joints, inspect screenshots and help you iterate. Manual editing remains available.

From idea to animation

Make a move.
Make it meaningful.

Designed around the creative journey, from the first pose to an animation you can bring into Second Life.

01

Start with a pose

Open an existing animation or create your own. Select a joint and shape the pose you have in mind.

02

Refine the motion

Work with keyframes and the timeline. Use manual controls or explore optional AI assistance.

03

Save and export

Save your work and export BVH for Second Life. Upload and playback are validated in Firestorm.
